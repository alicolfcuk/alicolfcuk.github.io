2026–27 Fall

Old Lisnave, Almada

Course
Architectural Design Studio
Site
Lisnave shipyard, Almada, Lisbon
Status
In progress

A mapping study of the abandoned Lisnave shipyard on the Tagus. The drawings read what a century of shipbuilding put into the ground, the water and the city across the river.

Carnation and Shipyard follows the yard from the 1974 revolution to its closure; Fractal Lisnave turns the site into a field guide of objects.

Course
Architectural Design Studio IV

    About
